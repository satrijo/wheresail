How it works

Three steps between you and a good decision.

Plot your route

Click waypoints on the map. The route is drawn leg by leg, with distance and estimated time at your boat speed.

Check every leg

Wind, waves and swell for each leg — not just at departure. A 25-knot headwind on leg three changes the whole day.

Sail the window

Slide the forecast timeline and find the hours when conditions agree with your plan. Then decide.

Built on open data

WhereSail runs on free, open forecast data — no keys, no paywall for the basics. Every layer names its source, and the map says plainly what it is: model guidance for planning, not a navigation chart and not an official forecast.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The map, the layers, the route planner and the 48-hour point forecast are free, with no account.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the map and sail — nothing to sign up for.

Is it a navigation chart?

No. WhereSail is a weather planning map. It shows forecast conditions, not depths, hazards or official navigation data. Always cross-check with official forecasts and charts before you go.

Where does the data come from?

Open forecast models — GFS via Open-Meteo and Copernicus Marine. Models are guidance, not gospel: conditions at sea can differ from the model, especially near the coast.
